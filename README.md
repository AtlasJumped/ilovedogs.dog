# ilovedogs.dog

Mood bands for dogs: green (come say hi), yellow (ask first, give space), red (keep your distance). Free bands, shipping only.

- index.html: vision, signals, free-band request form
- donate.html: transparency note, Wikipedia giving record, three tiers (buttons not live yet)
- js/site.js: entry pop-up and forms (Web3Forms to the team inbox)
- The HMR dock loads from hmrfamily.life/x/.
