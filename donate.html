Our mission: a world where every dog lover knows, at a glance, whether they can play with a dog or not.

In full transparency

We're a very small team who just wants to do good things for humanity. Everyone on the team works a full-time job, and we plan to keep doing that.

Everything it took to get here, we paid for ourselves, and we plan to keep doing that too. But it would be really nice if we didn't have to.

We put many hours a week into this on top of our jobs. If you give, it means we can keep the sets free, keep the sites running, and now and then reward ourselves for those hours. We appreciate that a lot.

We give to Wikipedia. Please give to them or to us.

Wikipedia is free, open and built for everyone, and it only lasts because people pay for it. Humanity needs to get to a place where open, free projects like this are rewarded, so they can keep going forever. So we give every month, and we ask the same of you: if you've ever used Wikipedia, please donate to Wikipedia or to us.
