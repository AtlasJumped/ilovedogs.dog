Sick dogs

A place to come when your dog is sick

We know people search the internet before they see a vet. So we want what they find to be good.

How it will work

We will use an open-source process and AI to gather what leading veterinary journals say, and what owners are saying on blogs and forums. Then, for each problem, one page in four parts:

  1. What blogs say. What owners report and recommend.
  2. What the medical evidence says. What the research shows, and how strong it is.
  3. What we think is probably going on. Our honest reading of the two.
  4. Where to check us. Every source, linked, so you and your vet can check our work.

This is information, not a diagnosis. Take it to your vet, and if your dog is in danger, go now.

Coming The first pages are being gathered.

An open question: mold and animals

One of us lost a dog who stopped eating. Several vets saw the dog, and none of them ever reached a diagnosis. We never found out why the dog died. We are curious whether mold exposure played a part.

We leave this here as an open question. We want a reliable database that vets and owners can both use, so fewer owners are left without answers.

If you are a vet or a researcher who works on this, we would love to hear from you through the volunteer form.
