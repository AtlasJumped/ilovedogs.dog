Help humanity feed its dogs well

Fresh food, open recipes, and no good food thrown away while dogs go without.

Kibble is not good for dogs

We strongly believe this, and we reject the narrative from anybody who says, as an expert, that kibble is good for dogs. It will sustain their life. But there is no way that processed food is good for dogs if the same kind of processed food is not good for humans.

Fresh-food companies are doing the right thing. Owners who switch often see better skin and fur, and a dog who loves dinner. These are some of the popular ones right now:

  • The Farmer's Dog
  • Ollie
  • Nom Nom
  • JustFoodForDogs

We are not affiliated with any of them, and nobody pays us to list them.

An open, honest source of dog-food recipes

The companies that forged the path keep their recipes proprietary. For a time there was a market for that, and we hope they have all made their money by now.

What the future needs is an open, honest, trusted source that anyone can go to for dog-food recipes, so you can make the best food for your dog yourself. We want to help make that happen.

Food rescue: what stores throw away, dogs could eat

Stores throw out food every day. If it is not feeding people who are homeless, it should be feeding dogs. Fresh fruit and vegetables that are bruised, or too unappealing for people but still human grade, can be sorted by ingredient, blended, and shared in the community.

  1. Stores post it. Location by location, a store manager tells us the day and time they throw food out, and where they put it.
  2. Collectors pick it up. A volunteer collects from about ten stores and brings it to one drop point.
  3. A driver gathers the drop points. Another volunteer picks those up with a truck and drives to a warehouse.
  4. Sorters sort it. Volunteers at the warehouse separate it: vegetable by vegetable, fruit by fruit, meat by meat.
  5. Dog owners pick it up free. People with dogs in that community drive over and pick up free food.

We know some people will take advantage of this, and we will engineer a way to make sure it reaches the right dogs. And if a human being who is hungry comes by, the protocol will say it is okay to give that food to people in need too.

Planned We are building the framework now. If you run a store or want to help, tell us on the volunteer form.
