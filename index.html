Mood bands for dogs

Can I play with your dog?

Our vision: a world where every dog lover knows, at a glance, whether they can play with a dog or not.

And a world where every owner of a dog that isn't friendly can tell everyone, from across the street, to keep their distance. If we all used the same simple signal, walks would be safer and happier for dogs and people.

The signals

One band on your dog answers the two questions every dog lover has: is this dog friendly? and is it OK to come say hi and play? We use traffic-light colours because almost everyone already knows them.

Green: come say hi

Friendly? Yes. Approach and play? Yes.

This dog loves people and other dogs. Ask the owner, then enjoy.

Yellow: ask first, give space

Friendly? Usually. Approach and play? Not right now.

Nervous, in training, healing or working. Please don't approach without asking.

Red: keep your distance

Friendly? No. Approach and play? No.

Please give this dog and its person plenty of room. Thank you.

These are guides, not guarantees. Always ask the owner before you touch any dog.

Our solution: very simple, very cheap bands

  • Bands that go around your dog's leg, made to any size, from tiny pups to giant breeds.
  • Collars in the same colours, for dogs who wear one every day.
  • Easy to see from a distance, so people know before they get close.
  • Easy to swap as your dog changes, from a nervous rescue to a happy green.
