Good owners

What some dog owners do that hurts every other owner

We want dogs allowed in more places. A few owners make that harder for all of us.

Why dogs get banned

Some owners will not follow the rules, and they make every dog owner look bad. Dogs are kept off beaches, and out of plenty of other places, for a few plain reasons:

  • Waste left behind. Nobody wants to step in it. Poop and pee left on sand, paths, and lawns are the first reason places say no.
  • Digging. Holes on a beach or in a park are a hazard and a mess.
  • Out of control. A dog that will not come back, jumps on strangers, or chases other animals ends it for everyone.

How to be the owner who wins access back

  • Always carry bags, and pick up every time, even where nobody is watching.
  • Fill in any hole your dog digs, and stop the digging early.
  • Keep your dog on a leash unless it comes back the first time you call, every time.
  • Ask before your dog greets a person or another dog. The colour code makes that easy.
  • Respect the rules where they exist, while we work to change the ones that make no sense.
